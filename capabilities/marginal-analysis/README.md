# Marginal Analysis

**Capability:** Constrained profit optimization using marginal-cost reasoning
(P ≈ MC) under price-taking conditions.

**Exercised in:** Perfect Competition case, Stages 1.2–1.3 — building and
auditing a constrained crop-mix optimization model, then using marginal-cost
analysis to explain the profit-maximizing crop mix and support a final business
recommendation.

**Contents of this folder:**
- `spec.md` — the model specification, written before the workbook existed,
  including validation rules and audit findings recorded after the build.
- `model.xlsx` — the completed Excel model, including Solver optimization,
  marginal-cost schedules, validation checks, and figures used to report the
  findings.

**Result:** 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds; seasonal
profit of $42,761.66, matching the case's published check figure of
approximately $42,762. The solution uses 60 of 64 available beds, with the
carrot and mesclun crop-specific caps binding.
