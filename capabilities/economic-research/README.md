# Economic Research

**Capability:** Independent economic analysis of a real-world challenge, from research question to defended policy or strategy recommendation.

## What this capability does

Takes a global or local challenge, explains it with course economics (micro, macro, or both), analyzes what the underlying mechanism does now and under named future conditions, and proposes a policy or strategy defended against the obvious objection. At least one figure carries evidence the argument actually uses.

## Method

Brief → Spec → Drafts → Log → Deliver

| Step | Artifact |
|---|---|
| 1. Ask | The challenge, the economic concepts it touches, and the planned analysis |
| 2. Plan | Data sources, model and figures, and success criteria (see `spec.md` in this folder) |
| 3. Draft | Dated snapshots, one per working session that moved the paper |
| 4. Log | Every AI session: what was asked, what came back, what I did with it |
| 5. Deliver | Final paper, figures, and closing reflection |

## Contents of this folder

- `spec.md`: research design, completed source register, implementation notes, and dated audit history; earlier plans remain in Git history
